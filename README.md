# plansafetrip

PlanSafeTrip is an AI-powered travel intelligence platform that helps travelers make better-informed decisions about destinations, neighborhoods, hotels, routes, scams, safety concerns, transport, hygiene, weather, and local alerts.

## The Problem

Travelers currently depend on fragmented information from review websites, social media, government advisories, blogs, and news sources.

This makes it difficult to understand the real situation in a specific neighborhood or location.

## The Solution

PlanSafeTrip combines trusted data, official advisories, maps, community insights, AI analysis, and real-time alerts to provide clear, location-specific travel intelligence.
